Our aim is to provide sophisticated structures for smart device casing and a great experience for users. Thus, we continuously invest in the development of fundamental technology platforms for design, simulation, materials, and processes, boost tooling and equipment machining capabilities, and facilitate automated production. Design for manufacturing Mass production Refined quality management Mechanics Cooling Antennas and Wireless Charging Hinge The increasing number of components in smart devices imposes a higher requirement on structural design and precision. We combine product lines of acoustics, optics, haptics, antennas, and mechanics onto a metal frame platform to enable more precise spatial design and manufacturing process. Therefore, we can provide customized solutions for a wide range of smart devices.
