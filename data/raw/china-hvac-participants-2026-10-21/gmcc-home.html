GMCC
    GMCC was founded in 1995 and has 7 intelligent factories and manufacturing centers,  7 R&D centers globally. The company focuses on the development, production, and sales of reciprocating compressors, rotary compressors, scroll compressors, electronic expansion valves, and other products. These products are widely used in various fields such as air conditioning, refrigeration, heat pumps, dehumidifiers, dryers, refrigerated vehicles, and water dispensers. GMCC is a comprehensive technology enterprise that integrates research and development, manufacturing, and marketing.     In terms of compressors, GMCC has fixed-speed and variable-frequency products that meet the basic needs of home appliances worldwide. The company continues to make progress in environmental protection, efficiency, small size, intelligence, and silence. GMCC has the leading market share in the air conditioning compressor market globally, with annual sales exceeding 100 million units, and ranks second in the refrigerator compressor market worldwide.
