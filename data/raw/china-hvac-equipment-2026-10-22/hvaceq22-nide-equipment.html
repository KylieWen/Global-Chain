Automatic Motor Production Lines

NIDE provides automated motor production lines for a wide range of motor applications, including EV motors, drone motors, hub motors, mid-drive motors, BLDC motors, universal motors, and induction motors. Our production lines integrate automated feeding, winding, inserting, forming, lacing, testing, and intelligent control into one efficient manufacturing solution. Flexible configurations can be customized to meet different motor designs, production capacities, and automation requirements.

Motor Manufacturing Equipment

NIDE offers a comprehensive range of motor manufacturing equipment covering stator production, rotor manufacturing, coil winding, coil insertion, forming, lacing, and testing processes. Designed with modular architecture, our equipment delivers high precision, stable performance, and flexible configurations for applications in electric vehicles, power tools, home appliances, industrial automation, and renewable energy industries.

Turnkey Motor Manufacturing Solutions

NIDE provides complete turnkey motor manufacturing solutions for customers worldwide. From process planning, equipment selection, and automated production line design to installation, commissioning, technical training, and after-sales service, we deliver comprehensive one-stop support. With extensive industry experience and proven automation technologies, we help manufacturers build efficient, reliable, and intelligent motor production facilities.

Motor Winding Solutions

NIDE provides professional motor winding solutions for various motor types, including needle winding, flyer winding, hairpin winding, stator winding, and integrated winding and insertion technologies. Our solutions are designed to meet different wire sizes, motor structures, and production requirements, delivering high efficiency, precision, and intelligent automation for EV motors, drone motors, industrial motors, and household appliance motors.
